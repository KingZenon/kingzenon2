01 / THE PROBLEM

Detect the crashes that matter most.

Fatal crashes may be a minority of incidents. Overall accuracy alone can hide missed fatal cases, so the main evaluation will report fatal-class recall together with precision.

01

Research question

Which model and imbalance strategy improves fatal-crash detection while keeping false alarms useful?

02

Factors

Examine the time, location, road, vehicle, and cause fields that are actually present in the data.

03

Generalisation

Train on earlier years and evaluate on later years to measure how performance changes over time.

03 / METHOD

Reproducible analysis workflow

This is the analysis specification. Completed steps and changes will be recorded in the progress log.

01

Prepare

Merge yearly files, harmonise names and categories, document missing values, and define a fatal versus non-fatal target.

02

Compare

Use a majority-class baseline, logistic regression, decision tree, and random forest. Compare class weights and tuned thresholds where supported.

03

Validate

Keep later years as a temporal holdout. Use cross-validation only within training years and report the exact split.

04

Interpret

Report fatal-class recall, precision, F1, PR-AUC, macro-F1, confusion matrices, and meaningful error patterns.

Potential hotspots can be explored after location coverage and data quality are checked. Any clustering method and evaluation will be documented with the results.
